8-Ball Pool Rules

  • Break Shot: Player 1 breaks first. Table remains Open until the first object ball is pocketed.
  • Group Assignment: Pocketing the first ball assigns you either Solids (1-7) or Stripes (9-15).
  • Turns: Legally pocket one of your assigned balls to gain another turn.
  • Scratch Foul: Pocketing the white cue ball or missing all balls grants opponent Ball-in-Hand (click table to place cue ball).
  • Winning: Clear all your assigned group balls, then legally pocket the 8-Ball into any pocket.
  • Instant Loss: Pocketing the 8-Ball prematurely or scratching on the 8-Ball loses the match instantly!
